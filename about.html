About

Welcome to the Survey Tools collection. This site provides a range of web applications designed to assist surveyors in their daily tasks.



Tools we currently offer:


  • GSI Viewer: View, analyze, and convert Leica GSI files from both Levels and Total Stations.
  • LSG Converter: Convert to and from London Survey Grid 2025.
  • Rail Analysis Dashboard: Visualise and analyse rail data purely from LHR and RHR survey data.
  • Resection Lab: Simulate the effect of geometry and instrument accuracies on resections.
  • DXF to CSV Extractor (beta): Easily convert DXF files to CSV format for data analysis.


Standalone Applications:


  • E57 Converter: Build virtual tours from 360 photographs, specifically designed for use in rail environments. Output in E57 format for use in viewers such as Hexagon GeoCloud
  • Asset Image Sequencer: Rename sequential images using a simple step-by-step process with built-in error detection.

More tools are coming soon, so stay tuned!
