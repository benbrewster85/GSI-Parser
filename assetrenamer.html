Asset Image Sequencer

Designed specifically for the renaming of sequential images, where a standard name + suffix process falls short, and manual renaming is time-consuming.


Utilising a simple step-by-step process with built in error detection, this lightweight tool takes a folder of images and an associated CSV file with names as input, replacing or creating copies of the original files, with original EXIF and Metadata intact.


Working particularly well with large datasets of sequential images, or in situations where naming files in a tabular (Excel) format is more convenient than renaming files one-by-one.



Features:


  • Rename files based on CSV data: Perfect for large datasets or photos in sequence.
  • Check your data: Built in alerts for inconsistencies in input data.
  • Remove duplicate shots: Tool interrogates imagery to alert where two photos taken within a short period of time.
  • Choose your output: Select whether to replace or create copies of the original files.
